Core of Light is a launcher for the recompiled Skylanders games (Spyro's Adventure, Giants,
Swap Force, Trap Team, SuperChargers, Imaginators).

It doesn't include any of the games or their data. You need your own Xbox 360 discs as ISO
files; the launcher's first-run setup asks for them and checks each one.

Your figures, saves and settings are kept in Documents\Skylanders and stay there when you
uninstall. The games are apps of their own: uninstall them in Windows Settings > Apps.

This is a test version and may contain bugs.
